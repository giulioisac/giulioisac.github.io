About

I develop unsupervised and generative learning methods to model immune receptor repertoires and to characterize how the adaptive immune system recognizes pathogens.

Previously a Postdoctoral Scientist at UC Berkeley and a Humboldt Research Fellow at the University of Leipzig, where I studied how bacterial and viral evolution drives immune evasion. I obtained my PhD jointly from ENS Paris and the Max Planck Institute for Dynamics and Self-Organization, Göttingen, on generative modeling of immune receptor repertoires.
